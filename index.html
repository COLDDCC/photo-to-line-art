Photo to line art with hatching

This free image-to-line-art converter uses parallel and crossed strokes to describe light and shade. Choose detailed hatching to retain photographic texture, or illustrative hatching for broader shapes and white highlights. Adjust line density, stroke width and contrast beside the original photo. Images are processed locally, and the output is a watermark-free PNG with a longest side up to 2000 pixels. This is a hatching generator, not an outline tracer, single-line drawing tool or SVG converter.

Before you download

Is this an outline converter?

This tool creates hatching: parallel or crossed strokes describe light and shadow. It does not trace outlines.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image processing happens in your browser. Your photos are not sent to a server.

What size is the download?

PNG downloads have a longest side up to 2000 pixels. Smaller originals are not enlarged.

Can I buy a handmade artwork?

Our artist section is separate from the converter. Purchase links will appear when the artist’s shop is available.
