A few things to know.

Is this an outline converter?

This tool creates hatching: parallel or crossed strokes describe light and shadow. It does not trace outlines.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Image processing happens in your browser. Your photos are not sent to a server.

What size is the download?

PNG downloads have a longest side up to 2000 pixels. Smaller originals are not enlarged.

Can I buy a handmade artwork?

Our artist section is separate from the converter. Purchase links will appear when the artist’s shop is available.
